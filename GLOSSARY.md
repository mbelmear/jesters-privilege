# Jester's Privilege

The website for Jester's Privilege, a weekly theology-and-humor podcast. It is both the show's front door and a place to spend time: Episodes, the history of fools, and the theology behind the motley.

## The show

**Jester**:
One of the three people who make the show: Michael, Dan, and Aaron. Each gets equal billing. Under their names, the site shows the labels they chose: "Co-host" for Michael and Dan, "Producer & Co-host" for Aaron. Their church roles (deacon, pastor) belong in their bios.
_Avoid_: Host, co-host, cast, team (anywhere but those labels)

**Fool**:
A historical court jester or holy fool featured in the Fools' Gallery, such as Stańczyk or Will Somers. Never one of the three Jesters. Lowercase "jester" may name the historical job ("Henry VIII's jester", "the jester's golden age"); as a name or label for a person, Jester means only Michael, Dan, or Aaron.
_Avoid_: Jester (as a name or label for a historical figure), clown

**Fools' Gallery**:
The part of the site that tells the history of jesters and holy fools as a timeline, each Fool shown with their painting.
_Avoid_: History page, jester history

**King**:
Christ, whom the Jesters serve. Never used for a person on the show or for the audience.
_Avoid_: Using "king" for listeners or leaders

**Episode**:
One published installment of the show, as it appears in the podcast feed.
_Avoid_: Show (for a single installment), post

**Show notes**:
The description written for an Episode when it is published to the podcast host, which the site displays as-is.
_Avoid_: Notes, description, summary

**Transcript**:
The machine-generated text of an Episode, shown on the Episode page.
_Avoid_: Captions, subtitles

**Listen link**:
A link to the show, or to one of its Episodes, in a podcast app (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube). On an Episode, it opens that Episode in the app; anywhere general, such as the footer, it opens the show. Listening on the site itself needs no Listen link: every Episode plays here, free.
_Avoid_: Platform link, subscribe button

## Paintings

**Painting band**:
A public-domain painting shown full width between reading sections, opening each era of the Fools' Gallery, with only a short title over it.
_Avoid_: Hero, banner

**Framed painting**:
A painting shown whole, in a frame and mat, beside a Fool's story.

**Placard**:
The museum label under a painting: artist, title, year, medium, and collection.
_Avoid_: Caption, credit

**Focal point**:
The point in a painting to keep in view when it is cropped, such as Stańczyk's face; given as fractions of the painting's width and height.

## Listener submissions

**Speak Freely**:
The page where a listener sends the Jesters a Submission, at `/speak-freely`. Its name is the jester's privilege itself: the license to speak freely before the king. The endpoint behind it keeps its plain name, `POST /api/submit`.
_Avoid_: Submit (as the page's name), contact form, mailbag

**Submission**:
Anything a listener sends the show through the site, whatever its type.
_Avoid_: Confession (as the umbrella term), entry, message, form response

**Question**:
A Submission asking the Jesters something they may answer on the show. The most common type.
_Avoid_: Listener question, Q&A

**Confession**:
A Submission in which a listener owns up to their own folly, for the Jesters to laugh about with them.
_Avoid_: Fool confession (in code and copy), story

**Topic idea**:
A Submission suggesting a subject for a future episode.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, pitch
