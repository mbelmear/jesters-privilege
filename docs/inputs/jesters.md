## Dan Collins

Church title: Discipleship Pastor

Dan is Redeemer's Discipleship Pastor. His call to ministry came about as the Lord was calling him back to himself. When he came back to Christ, he also came back to the church and back to reading the word of God. There it became abundantly clear to him that his life, whatever he was going to do, had to revolve around understanding and proclaiming the word of God. This led him to enrolling in Bible college, where God confirmed his call into ministry. Dan has served in churches both around the country and around the world. Dan and his wife, Annie, have five beautiful children. Dan is excited to be moving to an area that is growing and changing, and to live in a place where he can help meet growing spiritual needs. He enjoys the outdoors whether that is hiking, camping, or his all-time favorite...mountain biking.

## Aaron Canen

Church title: Technical and Communications Director

Aaron was raised in Glendive, MT where he met his wife, Mara. They were married in the Glendive church he grew up in, where his father served as pastor. He and Mara studied at Montana State University, where Aaron received his Bachelor's degree in Music Technology. Aaron has a passion for facilitating the intersection of art and technology. He has three children, Aayla, Abel, and Audrey.
